📘 Learn: English Text Types

Recount Text

Retells past experiences or events in chronological order.

  • Purpose: retell past events.
  • Structure: Orientation → Events → Reorientation.
  • Language: simple past, action verbs, time connectors.
Example: Last Saturday, our class visited a motorcycle workshop. First, we observed the mechanic. Then, we helped clean the tools. Finally, we discussed what we learned.

Procedure Text

Explains how to do or make something: Goal → Materials → Steps.

Narrative Text

Tells a story: Orientation → Complication → Resolution.
